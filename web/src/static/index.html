Look, here's the deal

We've all been there. You just need to know when the bloody train is coming. Not the weather. Not traffic updates. Not suggestions for restaurants you'll never visit.

Just... when's the train? That's it. That's literally all you want. So we made an app that does exactly that. Revolutionary, we know.

One glance at your home screen and you're done. No opening apps. No waiting for them to load. No faff. Just the information you actually need, right there, instantly. Like magic, but boring and practical.

What's in the box?

⚡

Real-time updates

Live departure times for Underground, Overground, DLR, and more. Updated faster than you can say "signal failure at Camden Town."

📍

Home screen widgets

Stick your favourite station right on your home screen. Check departures without unlocking your phone. It's almost too easy.

🔔

Smart alerts

We'll tell you about delays and service changes. Because finding out the Northern Line is suspended when you're already on the platform is the worst.

Widget life, best life

Slap the Stationly widget on your home screen and never open the app again. (We mean that in a good way.)

See live departure times right there, next to your embarrassing number of unread emails. Perfect for morning panic, evening commutes, or those "should I run for it?" moments.

Works on Android and iOS. Because we're not monsters who play favourites. And yes, it updates faster than you can say "signal failure at Camden Town."
