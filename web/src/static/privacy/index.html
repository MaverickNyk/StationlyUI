Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 April 2026  ·  Effective: 26 April 2026

1. Who We Are

Stationly ("Stationly", "we", "us", "our") is an independent software developer operating the Stationly mobile application, home screen widget, and website at stationly.co.uk (collectively the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

We are committed to handling your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. For all privacy-related enquiries, please contact us at info@stationly.co.uk.

2. Data We Collect

Category Data Why We Collect It Legal Basis
Account Name, email address Identifying and securing your account Contract
Station preferences Saved stations, lines, directions Syncing your boards across devices Contract

We do not collect your GPS or precise location at any point. All station selection is made manually by you within the app. We do not collect payment information. We do not build advertising profiles. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party under any circumstances.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data only for the following purposes, and no others:

  • Creating, authenticating, and maintaining your account
  • Synchronising your saved station preferences across your devices
  • Delivering push notifications containing departure data for your saved stations
  • Diagnosing technical problems and crashes
  • Improving the Service through anonymised usage analysis
  • Responding to support requests and enquiries
  • Complying with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the Service

4. Third-Party Sub-Processors

To deliver the Service, your data may be processed by the following third parties. Each processes data under their own privacy policies, over which we have no control:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics) — core infrastructure for accounts, data storage, push notifications, and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — optional social authentication. Subject to Google Privacy Policy
  • Transport for London (TfL) Open Data API — source of all departure data. API requests are made without transmitting your identity, account details, or station preferences to TfL

We do not use any advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing analytics platforms. We do not share your personal data with any other third party except as required by law.

5. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed on Google Firebase servers located in the United States and within the European Economic Area. Google LLC relies on Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA) to ensure lawful transfer of personal data outside the UK. For more information, see Firebase Privacy & Security.

We accept no liability for the data handling practices of Google or any other third-party sub-processor, including any failure of their security measures or any data breach on their systems.

6. Data Retention

  • Account and preference data — retained for the duration your account is active and deleted within 30 days of account deletion
  • Push notification tokens — deleted when you revoke notification permissions or uninstall the app
  • Crash logs and device data — retained for up to 12 months in anonymised or aggregated form
  • Support emails — retained for up to 2 years for reference and compliance purposes
  • Optional profile data — deleted immediately upon account deletion or upon your request

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption of all data in transit via HTTPS/TLS
  • Encryption at rest via Google Firebase infrastructure
  • Firebase Security Rules that restrict database access to authenticated account holders only
  • No storage of passwords in plain text (managed entirely by Firebase Authentication)
  • Restricted internal access to personal data on a need-to-know basis

However, no system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data. To the fullest extent permitted by law, we accept zero liability for any data breach, unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure of personal data, however caused, including as a result of our own negligence or the failure of any third-party system.

In the event of a data breach affecting your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) and, where required, you, within the timeframes required by UK GDPR.

8. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data. To exercise any right, contact us at info@stationly.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days. We reserve the right to verify your identity before processing any request.

  • Right of access — to obtain a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — to request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal retention obligations
  • Right to restrict processing — to request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability — to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — to object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — to withdraw consent at any time for any processing based on consent (including push notifications via your device settings), without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
  • Right to complain — to escalate concerns to the relevant data protection authority if you believe your data has not been handled lawfully

Note that some rights are not absolute and may be subject to limitations under applicable law. We accept no liability for any delay or failure to respond to a rights request caused by circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

9. Automated Decision-Making and Profiling

We do not use any automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on you. No decisions about you are made solely by automated means. We do not build any behavioural, demographic, or interest-based profiles from your data.

10. Special Category Data

We do not knowingly collect, process, or store any special category personal data as defined by UK GDPR, including data relating to racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data, health data, sex life, or sexual orientation. If you believe you have inadvertently shared such data with us, contact us at info@stationly.co.uk and we will delete it promptly.

11. Marketing Communications

We will not send you marketing or promotional communications without your explicit prior consent. Where we send service-related communications (such as account notifications or changes to these policies), these are not marketing and are necessary for the operation of the Service. You may opt out of any marketing communication at any time by contacting us at info@stationly.co.uk.

12. Do Not Track

Our website does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals, as there is no industry-standard interpretation of DNT. However, as stated in this Policy, we do not use any cross-site tracking, advertising cookies, or behavioural analytics. Your browsing of this website is not tracked for advertising purposes regardless of your DNT setting.

13. Data Protection Officer

As a small independent developer, we are not required under UK GDPR to appoint a Data Protection Officer. All data protection matters are handled directly by the company at info@stationly.co.uk.

14. Firebase Analytics

The Stationly app may use Firebase Analytics (Google) to collect anonymised, aggregated usage data such as screen views and feature interactions. This data is collected without any personally identifying information and is used solely to understand how the app is used in aggregate in order to improve the Service. You may opt out of Firebase Analytics data collection by adjusting your device's advertising settings or by contacting us.

15. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website uses only the following browser storage mechanisms:

  • Firebase Authentication tokens — stored in browser local storage solely to maintain your signed-in session. These are functional and not advertising cookies.
  • Session state — tab navigation state is held in memory only and is not persisted to storage.

We do not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or any form of cross-site tracking. We do not use Google Analytics or any similar analytics platform on this website.

16. Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data without your consent, please contact us at info@stationly.co.uk and we will delete such data promptly. We accept no liability for any harm arising from the use of the Service by individuals under 13 who have provided false information about their age.
